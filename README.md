🧠 MindFul

MindFul is a full-stack web-based mood and journaling system designed to help users record their daily experiences, monitor personal well-being, review their journal history, and interact with an AI Assistant for self-reflection.

The platform provides dedicated functionalities for both users and administrators. Users can manage their personal journal entries, view their personal dashboard, review their history, and communicate with the AI Assistant. Administrators can oversee system-wide information through analytics, manage users, review journal records, and monitor AI Assistant activity.

# 🔄 System Workflow

1. Visitors arrive at the public Homepage (Home, About, Services, Contact Us).
2. Users or administrators create an account.
3. Users can sign in using their credentials or Google Authentication.
4. Users access their personal Dashboard.
5. Users create Daily Journal entries containing their mood and personal information.
6. Journal entries are stored and associated with the user's account.
7. Users can view their previous entries through History.
8. Users can interact with the AI Assistant using their personal MindFul information.
9. Administrators can access system-wide Analytics.
10. Administrators can manage registered users through User Management.
11. Administrators can review journal records through Journal Management.
12. Administrators can monitor AI conversations through AI Assistant Management.
13. Visitors can send inquiries through the Contact Us form.
